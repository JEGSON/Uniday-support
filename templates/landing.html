Whatever is on your mind, we can start there.

University life can bring a lot at once. If something is making things harder, you can tell us in your own words.

FIND YOUR STARTING POINT

What do you need help with today?

Choose a topic to find out a little more. It is okay if you are not sure yet.

Wellbeing

Talk to us about how you are feeling or anything affecting your wellbeing.

Academic

Ask about challenges with study, coursework or your university experience.

Financial

Let us know if money worries are affecting your studies or daily life.

Disability

Ask about support related to a disability or access needs.

Accommodation

Tell us about concerns with your housing or living arrangements.

Something else

If your concern does not fit a category, you can still tell us about it.

ONE STEP AT A TIME

What happens when you reach out?

You do not need to have everything figured out before you get in touch.

  1. 01

    Tell us what is going on

    Write a request in your own words and choose the topic that feels closest.

  2. 02

    We find the right person

    A member of the support team is assigned to your request.

  3. 03

    Keep in touch

    Sign in to see updates and reply when you are ready.

  4. 04

    Find a way forward

    Work with the support team towards a next step that helps.

YOUR INFORMATION MATTERS

Who can see your request?

Your request is intended for you and the university staff authorised to support you. Share only what you feel is relevant, and check your university’s privacy information for details of how your information is used.

WHEN YOU ARE READY

You do not have to work it out alone.

Start with a few words. We will take it from there.
